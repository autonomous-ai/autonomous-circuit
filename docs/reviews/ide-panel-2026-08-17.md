## Fixed the same day

Every lens's must-fix list was worked the day it landed, which is why the
round's write-up and the code no longer agree — deliberately.

- **Editing.** Identity now comes before position: a placement is found by its
  `name` prop, which survives an insertion, and only by ordinal when there is
  no name. A re-pointed ordinal is refused *by name* instead of written. A
  render test builds the judge's exact case and asserts the part the engineer
  never selected does not move. The false `AGENT_ONLY` reason was replaced with
  what is actually true — nothing calls `board_edit_apply` — plus why it is
  kept and what should happen if that stops being a reason.
- **Verdict.** Both severity tiers are counted and both are on screen. The kind
  classifier is a prefix rule plus a named rest, and a test walks every sidecar
  in the repo and fails on any kind the build emits that it does not recognise
  — measured against the artifacts, never against memory. `atEpochS` was parsed
  and never shown; the chip now says "the last full build (12 min ago) found…".
  The latency claim is re-timed and re-worded.
- **Integrity.** A swap is caught by calibrating the board against itself:
  placements sharing a tag must bind to the same *shape* of refdes, so a
  `<resistor>` bound to `LED+R` is bound to something it is not. What it still
  cannot catch — two placements of the *same* tag exchanged — is stated as a
  limit rather than papered over. `SOURCE_CHANGED` now names the collision.
- **Familiarity.** Double-click zooms to what is under the cursor and fits the
  board only from empty space. `F11` toggles Properties, `Tab` puts the caret
  in the selected part's X box. The shortcut sheet gained a `note` field
  carrying every place our key means something other than Altium's.
- **Discoverability.** Thirty-three plain-language entries, and the dictionary
  is now measured against the fleet rather than remembered — the test found the
  last four codes itself.
- **Board readiness.** `scripts/install-fleet` syncs every product into the
  app, and `scripts/fleet-status` prints the repo count and the app count side
  by side, plus a flag for any board whose sidecar is older than its own
  source. Two numbers that can part company will part company; the fix is to
  print them together.

## The bar, and what is between us and it

The ship bar: every lens ≥ 7, Familiarity ≥ 8 and Editing ≥ 8, zero must-fix
open, three example boards at ≤ 3 human edits each and every one a judgement
call.

Two lenses are under 7, and both for reasons now fixed but not yet re-driven —
**a fix is not a score until someone who did not write it says so**. What is
genuinely open:

1. **The USB pair, ledger #12, third round running.** All three example boards
   carry it: 10.39mm, 5.87mm and 20.25mm of skew against a 3.8mm budget, and on
   terminal-keyboard one segment never got a corridor at all. This is the last
   defect standing between the examples and "≤3 edits, all judgement".
2. **`rp2040-core`'s internal fanout**, ledger #53. Four independent engineers
   have now hit the identical signature — a via inside or hard against the
   QFN's 0.4mm-pitch pad field — and every board-level lever is measured and
   ruled out: 5x, the automatic 10x escalation, a hand-declared 10x, and a
   wider block gap (which at 9mm made a board *worse*). It is inside the block.
3. **ERC findings have no crosshair.** 23% of a real sidecar cannot be located
   from the message, and the coordinate exists.
4. **No app menu off Windows**, unfixed for three rounds.
