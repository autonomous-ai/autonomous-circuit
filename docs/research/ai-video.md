# AI Video Generation Landscape — Aug 2026 (platform / fal-orchestration view)

Scope: what the Autonomous TV "film crew" should route to via fal.ai for vertical 9:16 short drama, aiming at cinematic. This complements our infra team's OPEN-model self-host doc (`projects/videogen-infra/model-lineup-2026-08-08.md`); here the lens is the CLOSED frontier we rent per-second through the aggregator, plus the technique upgrades that cut the 5:1 抽卡 (gacha) retry tax. All prices/dates verified this session against primary sources; where a fetch was blocked I say so.

## The tier map (Aug 2026)

**Frontier, native-audio, drama-shaped (route the money here):**
- **Kling 3.0 / v3-video** (Kuaishou) — the single most drama-shaped model right now. **15s max, and a `multi_prompt` MULTI-SHOT mode: up to 6 connected shots in ONE generation, each with its own prompt + duration summing to the total.** Native audio = dialogue WITH lip-sync + SFX + ambient, best in EN/CN. Start image + optional end image. 720p (std) / 1080p (pro). Verified: https://replicate.com/kwaivgi/kling-v3-video . Series launched Jan 31 2026; **v3.0 Omni** adds reference-based editing, character extraction and a "subject library" (主体库) for cross-shot character lock (our infra doc, model-usage.md). Third-party API price for the 2.6 line was ~$0.07–0.14/s (https://aiworkflows.tools/blog/best-ai-tools-for-short-drama-2026).
- **Seedance 2.5** (ByteDance) — **native 30-second generation** (set 4–30s, or -1 for auto), joint audio+video (not a separate pass), **combine up to 30 reference images** in one gen, and last-frame control ("specify a last frame image to control where the video ends up"). Verified: https://replicate.com/bytedance/seedance-2.5 . Seedance 2.0 (the workhorse below it) takes 9 img + 3 video + 3 audio refs with lip-sync; Volcengine API ≈ 1 yuan/s ≈ $0.14/s (https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-04/14/content_502009.html). This is our long-take + character-lock engine.
- **Veo 3.1** (Google) — native audio (dialogue/SFX/ambient), 1080p and 4K, 8s base with **scene extension** for longer sequences, **first/last-frame** transitions, reference images for character consistency, add/remove objects, camera controls. Verified features: https://deepmind.google/models/veo/ . **Exact Gemini-API pricing:** standard $0.40/s (720p+1080p), $0.60/s (4K); **Fast** $0.10/s (720p) / $0.12/s (1080p) / $0.30/s (4K); **Lite** $0.05/s (720p) / $0.08/s (1080p) — audio included, only charged on success. https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing . On fal, Veo 3.1 / Fast / Lite are listed.
- **MiniMax Hailuo — H3 (Hailuo 3.0)** — omni-modal single transformer, native STEREO audio, 2K@24fps, 4–15s (extendable ~30s), 9 img + 3 vid + 3 audio refs. API $0.13/s at 2K, $0.09/s at 768p beta (our model-usage.md; https://huggingface.co/blog/ResterChed/minimax-h3-hailuo-3-0). Hailuo **2.3** (on fal/replicate) is the "expressive characters + cinematic VFX + realistic human motion" option: https://replicate.com/minimax .
- **Vidu Q3** (Shengshu) — explicitly "the first video model born for drama." 16s, 1080p, **start-end-to-video mode**, synchronized audio, strong reference-to-video for multi-entity consistency; drama partners include Sohu/Tencent动漫/iQIYI/Mango. Verified: https://replicate.com/vidu ; https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260415A04CEN00 . Best for the long DIALOGUE shot.

**Frontier, motion/physics leaders (photoreal hero shots):**
- **Runway Gen-4.5** — #1 on Artificial Analysis text-to-video (Elo 1247, above Veo 3 and Sora 2 Pro), released Dec 1 2025; SOTA motion quality, prompt adherence, physics; photoreal↔animated range. No native audio called out. Verified: https://runway.com/research/introducing-runway-gen-4.5 . Known weak spots they admit: causal-reasoning, object permanence, success-bias.
- **Luma Ray 3.2** — cinematic 5–10s, native HDR/EXR export (a real finishing-pipeline advantage). https://replicate.com/collections/text-to-video .

**Open / self-hostable (for our own GPU plane, and for cost floor):**
- **Wan 2.7** (Alibaba) — t2v up to 15s @1080p WITH audio; i2v with frame control + audio sync; **r2v** (identity-preserving reference-to-video); **videoedit** (natural-language edits). https://replicate.com/wan-video . Wan 2.2 is Apache-2.0 and already in our infra stack.
- **LTX-2 / 2.3** (Lightricks) — 4K@50fps native audio, portrait support, **retake (regional/section editing — "rephrase, change the action, camera angles")**, extend, camera controls; **ltx-2-distilled is open-weight** ("first open source audio-video model"). https://replicate.com/lightricks .
- **HunyuanVideo 1.5**, **Grok Imagine Video** (xAI, ~30s-gen fast synced audio), **PixVerse v5.6** round out the cheap/fast floor.

**Image/consistency engines that feed video (we already have most):** Seedream 5 Pro (up to 10 reference images, 1K/2K), Seedream 4.5/4 (4K edit), nano-banana, Flux — https://replicate.com/bytedance . These are the character-sheet + first-frame generators the video models key off.

## The critical anti-steal: Sora 2 is a dead end
OpenAI's live dev docs still serve Sora 2 today ($0.10/s std at 720×1280; Sora 2 Pro $0.30–0.70/s; snapshots sora-2-2025-12-08 default and sora-2-2025-10-06; https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/sora-2). BUT the **Sora API is scheduled for discontinuation Sept 24, 2026** (app already closed Apr 26 2026; economics ~$1M/day cost vs ~$2.1M total revenue) — https://the-decoder.com/openai-sets-two-stage-sora-shutdown-with-app-closing-april-2026-and-api-following-in-september/ . Do NOT build routing or a "crew role" on Sora; it sunsets in ~6 weeks.

## Long-coherent-scene techniques (the real craft upgrade)
The retry tax is the whole cost story (up to 70% of a drama's budget; 5–6 gens per usable shot — model-usage.md). Four levers, in order of leverage:
1. **Native long-take routing** — stop assuming 5–8s clips. Seedance 2.5 does 30s, Vidu Q3 16s, Kling v3 / Wan 2.7 15s natively. One long gen beats stitching four.
2. **Multi-shot in ONE generation** — Kling v3 `multi_prompt` (6 shots, per-shot prompt+duration) keeps cast/lighting/world identical across cuts for free, because it's one gen. Wan's 智能分镜 storyboard is the open analog.
3. **Last-frame chaining** — for takes longer than any single model: pull the final frame of clip N, pass it as the START image of the next i2v clip, and use END-frame guidance (Kling / Seedance 2.5 / Vidu start-end / Wan first-last-frame) to land on a known pose. This is how you get arbitrarily long coherent takes with hard continuity.
4. **Regional RETAKE instead of full reroll** — LTX-2 `retake`, Kling Omni edit, Wan `videoedit` fix one region/action/camera without regenerating the whole shot. This directly attacks the 5:1 抽卡 multiplier.
Plus: **reference-set character lock** (Seedance 2.5's 30 imgs, Kling subject library, Seedream 5's 10 refs, Wan r2v, or open character-LoRA) for cross-episode face consistency; and **native-audio-first routing** so most dialogue shots skip our separate sync-lipsync stage entirely.

Directly cuts the 5:1 retry tax that is ~70% of drama budget. (https://replicate.com/bytedance/seedance-2.5)
- [M] Add Kling v3 multi_prompt as a 'multi-shot scene in one generation' crew primitive (up to 6 connected shots, per-shot prompt+duration, native lip-sync audio) — Cuts inside one generation keep lighting/world/cast identical for free (no cross-clip drift), and native dialogue+SFX+ambient means no separate lip-sync pass. This is the single most drama-shaped closed model available and is our default hook/reveal engine. (https://replicate.com/kwaivgi/kling-v3-video)
- [M] Implement last-frame chaining for takes longer than any single model: extract final frame -> start image of next i2v clip, with end-frame guidance to land the pose — Gives arbitrarily long coherent takes with hard continuity; end-frame control exists on Kling v3, Seedance 2.5, Vidu Q3 start-end, and Wan first-last-frame, so it's a routing/orchestration change, not new model work. (https://replicate.com/vidu)
- [M] Add a regional RETAKE path (LTX-2 retake / Kling Omni edit / Wan videoedit) so 'make her angrier / fix the camera' edits one region instead of rerolling the whole shot — Attacks the 5-6-gens-per-usable-shot gacha multiplier head-on; a surgical edit costs a fraction of a full reroll and preserves everything already approved in the shot.
