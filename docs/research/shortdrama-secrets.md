=== 2. HOOK SCIENCE (first 0–3 seconds) ===
Three canonical cold-open patterns — a hit opens on exactly one, never on an establishing shot or slow entrance:
1. THE FACE — tight close-up on a character mid-reaction (shock/betrayal/fear).
2. THE SHOCK LINE — one line that reframes everything ("She's not your daughter.").
3. THE REVEAL IN MOTION — camera discovering evidence with no lead-up.
Ad-world variant (used to cut the ad creative itself): "drop the twist immediately," e.g. "He's cheating on me… with my boss?" Best practice is to generate 3–4 hook options at premium quality and pick. https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-hooks-cliffhangers/ , https://xmp.mobvista.com/en-blog/docs/How-to-Win-Big-with-Short-Drama-Ads-on-TikTok

=== 3. EPISODE MICROSTRUCTURE (the beat clock) ===
Per 60–120s episode (target ~90s, 120–180 words of dialogue+action):
- 0–3s Cold Open / hook
- 5–15s restate stakes
- 15–60s escalation — exactly ONE confrontation + ONE power shift (one reversal per 60s max; write reversals as visual beats, not dialogue)
- 60–85s turn/reveal
- 85–90s cliffhanger — cut one beat BEFORE resolution
Discipline rule that protects completion rate: "one episode = one escalation + one turn." https://www.minionarts.com/blogs/ai-microdrama-script-structure-cliffhanger-formula , https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-hooks-cliffhangers/

=== 4. CLIFFHANGER TAXONOMY (卡点) ===
Four types, rotate emotional tone across consecutive episodes so it never feels repetitive:
- THE REVEAL — viewer sees what character hasn't; cut before reaction
- THE THREAT — danger enters but doesn't land (car accelerating, hand on shoulder)
- THE REVELATION — character learns something; end on their reaction
- THE DECISION — binary choice presented; cut before they choose
Genre-specific cuts: romance=interrupted intimacy, revenge=recognition moment, CEO=public-humiliation reversal, supernatural=transformation reveal. https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-hooks-cliffhangers/ , https://www.minionarts.com/blogs/ai-microdrama-script-structure-cliffhanger-formula

=== 5. PAYWALL PLACEMENT (付费点) — the money mechanic ===
- Default boundary: episodes 8–10; the FIRST paid episode must OPEN with the season's first major reveal. Unlock $0.50–$1.00 (per-ep $0.30–0.50; full series $10–15). Later paywalls cluster at eps 26–30 and ~60. https://www.minionarts.com/blogs/ai-microdrama-script-structure-cliffhanger-formula , https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1900588157344416179
- The Chinese craft rule is deeper than an episode number: place the wall at the EMOTIONAL BEAT, not the count. Split each episode into 4 beats — (1) setup=weakest, (2) rising tension=too early, (3) critical moment right before the twist=OPTIMAL, (4) resolution/release=worst (relief → drop-off). "Cut when the fist is raised but hasn't fallen." High-conflict genres (revenge/face-slap/mystery) can wall earlier; slow-burn (family/romance) must deliver the first payoff free. A wall with no preceding hook = "a cold barrier that causes abandonment." https://www.dramwa.com/blog/duanju-fufei-kadian-zenme-she

=== 6. SEASON ARCHITECTURE ===
60–100 eps, 5 acts ~12 eps each. Act 1 (eps 1–10) = free, build attachment. Act 2 escalate premise. Acts 3–4 betrayal/reversal cycles with a FALSE VICTORY at eps 30–35. Act 5 resolution + sequel hook. https://www.minionarts.com/blogs/ai-microdrama-script-structure-cliffhanger-formula
