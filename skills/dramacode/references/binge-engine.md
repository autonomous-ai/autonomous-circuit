# The binge engine — the secret sauce, in one system

**Load when:** starting any new series, when a draft is competent but "not bingeable",
when the user says "make it more bingeable / bingeable / I can't stop watching", or when
scoring a series for the eval. This is the meta-doctrine that unifies the individual craft
patterns (hook, face-slap, cliffhanger, paywall) into the one machine that makes short
dramas compulsive — and makes that machine **measurable**. Load it *first* for a series;
the beat law and genre packs execute underneath it.

## The claim: binge is engineered, and it's one loop

Short dramas aren't bingeable by accident and it isn't the production value (it's often
cheap). It's a **compulsion loop** run at a punishing cadence — the same loop that powers
slot machines and feeds, married to a soap opera's emotional investment. Netflix has story
but rations emotion; TikTok has dopamine but no story to inhabit. Short drama is the hybrid:
**instant, repeatable emotional payoff inside a story you can live in.** Name the loop, run
it every episode, and the series is bingeable by construction.

## The loop (run it every episode, escalate it across the series)

```
  HOOK (0-3s)  →  ESCALATION  →  虐 setup  →  爽 payoff  →  CLIFFHANGER (last 6-10s)
   seize the       raise the      inflict the   detonate the   cut on the worst/
   scroll          stakes fast    injustice     reversal +     best moment;
      ▲                           (torment)     face-slap      never resolve
      │                                              │              │
      └──────────── variable reward ────────────────┘              ▼
         (you don't know how big the next payoff is)      the next episode is
                                                          the only relief → autoplay/pay
```

Across the series the same loop compounds: the **satisfaction ladder** escalates each
payoff's *scope* (personal → social → institutional → total), the **parasocial bond**
deepens over 50 episodes, and the **paywall** sits at the cliffhanger where craving peaks.

## The eight forces (this is the secret sauce, itemized)

Each is a lever the tools must supply by default; each maps to a measurable signal.

1. **The 3-second hook (黄金3秒).** Open mid-conflict — a slap, "sign the divorce papers,"
   a reveal. Create a question the brain must answer. *Signal: is there a stakes-question by
   3s? (ref `patterns/cold-open-hook.md`)*
2. **爽 (shuǎng) payoff cadence.** Visceral catharsis — the face-slap (打脸), the underdog
   rise (逆袭), the hidden-identity reveal — served every 60-90s, not rationed over a season.
   *Signal: 爽点 density (payoffs per minute). (ref `patterns/face-slap-cascade.md`)*
3. **虐→爽 (torment→payoff) pairing — and the RATIO.** Catharsis scales with the grievance set
   up right before it; the pleasure is the *contrast*. The mega-hit teardown sharpens this: the
   binge lever is the **虐/爽 ratio and proximity**, not payoff *count* — hits over-invest in
   torment (the free run can be almost pure humiliation-stacking) so the first big 打脸 detonates
   with *episodes* of accrued pressure behind it. Default: a grievance-heavy opening (small wins
   only), the first MAJOR payoff withheld to the paywall beat. Never a payoff without its setup;
   never sustained despair without a small win. *Signal: 虐 accrued before the first major 爽.*
4. **The per-episode cliffhanger.** Every episode ends on the worst/most shocking moment;
   resolution always lives in the next episode. *Signal: does every episode end unresolved,
   in the last 6-10s? (ref `patterns/cliffhanger-beat.md`)*
5. **Variable reward.** The size/timing of the next payoff is slightly unpredictable — a big
   face-slap or a smaller beat — so the viewer keeps going. *Signal: payoff sizes vary, not a
   flat metronome.*
6. **Wish-fulfillment for the disrespected.** The self-insert protagonist is invisible/wronged/
   underestimated and gains dignity, wealth, love, vengeance. Emotional restitution for people
   who feel unseen. *Signal: is there a clear self-insert wound in ep 1, paid off progressively?*
7. **Parasocial + low cognitive load.** Big faces, near-camera intimacy, emotions named aloud,
   clean morality, consistent cast across 50 eps → a *relationship* you can follow half-
   attentively on mute. *Signal: cast consistency (load-bearing here, not cosmetic) + muted-
   legibility + a bonded lead. (ref `emotion-to-action.md`, `emotional-core.md`)*
8. **The dramatic-irony gap (the single most repeatable device).** From ~ep 2 the *audience*
   knows the protagonist's hidden truth — power / identity / wealth / foreknowledge — that the
   on-screen tormentors don't. Every scene after is *loaded*: you watch knowing the reckoning is
   coming, which is what makes each humiliation bingeable rather than bleak (it's an IOU, not
   just pain). Reveal the secret to the viewer early, keep it from the villains, and peel it in
   escalating "vests" (马甲) — each face-slap exposing it to a bigger audience. *Signal: is the
   hidden truth revealed to the audience but withheld from the antagonists by ~ep 2? (ref
   `docs/hit-teardowns.md`)*

## The binge score (make it measurable)

Craft quality is scored by the screening-room film rubric. **Binge is a separate axis** —
a series can be beautifully made and un-bingeable. Score these 0-10 (the eval will own the
canonical version; this is the shared definition):

| dimension | what it measures | strong (9-10) |
|---|---|---|
| `hook_strength` | the 3s open across ep 1 + every episode | a stakes-question at t=0, every episode |
| `payoff_cadence` | 爽点 density — payoffs per minute | a payoff every 60-90s; none missing its 虐 |
| `cliffhanger_pull` | how hard each ending forces the next tap | every ep ends unresolved on the peak beat |
| `reversal_cadence` | reversals per episode + escalation across the series | ≥1/ep, scope escalating personal→total |
| `wish_fulfillment` | a clear self-insert wound + progressive restitution | ep-1 wound, paid off on the satisfaction ladder |
| `bingeability` | variable reward + parasocial bond + consistency | you'd start the next episode without deciding to |
| `clip_ability` | liftable ad-hooks (the funnel; see `patterns/ad-cut-sheet.md`) | several standalone scroll-stoppers per series |

**Overall binge** is a judgment, not an average — a dead hook or an episode that resolves
itself caps it low regardless of the rest. A great story with a low binge score gets the
note "make it compulsive," not "make it better."

## Packaging it for a non-producer (the whole point)

The creator supplies **premise + taste**; the tool supplies **the machine.** They should never
see "reversal cadence" — they answer *who's the fantasy for, what's the wound, what do they
want*, and the tool instantiates the loop: picks a genre engine (`genre-playbook.md`), plants
the ep-1 wound, schedules the 爽点 ladder and cliffhangers across 50 episodes, marks the paywall
at the craving peak (`patterns/paywall-gate-episode.md`), and cuts the ad-hooks. The creator
steers by feel ("make her angrier", "I want him to grovel here") — the binge is default-on.

## Pitfalls (the binge-killers)

- **A payoff with no setup** — a face-slap that lands with no prior humiliation is noise, not
  爽. Plant the 虐 first.
- **Resolving inside the episode** — the single most common binge-killer. Cut on the wound.
- **A flat payoff metronome** — same-size beat every 30s numbs; vary the reward.
- **No self-insert wound** — spectacle with no one to *be* is watchable, not compulsive.
- **Cast drift** — break the face across episodes and the parasocial bond (the thing that pulls
  someone through 50 episodes) collapses. Consistency is an binge lever, not a polish item.
- **Competent-but-quiet** — the failure mode of "good taste": a well-made episode that never
  spikes. The score catches it; the fix is more loop, not more polish.
